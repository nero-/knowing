The headers of `claims.csv`/`numbers.csv` and the shape of `.verify/faithfulness.json`/`qualifiers.json` are the canon of `source_scoring.md`/`runtime_verification.md`, the gate fails any other variant.

- Phase 5.5: do not rewrite `sources/NN.md` (archive), filter by the relevance of the fragment to the claim, not by `total`. Phase 6.5: do not trust the presence of a link — entailment on the verbatim quote; verdicts in `.verify/*.json`, do not recompute them in rubric/F10; take pairs from `evidence/`, do not rescan `sources/`; fix the report, not the ledger. A named framework/«law» — only with `[sNN]` or a «our frame» mark. No more than 25 words verbatim from a single source in the report; the limits do not stack into one long block.
- Synthesis: do not write the report in one pass over the pool; do not leave a `triangulated`/`contested` claim outside `outline.md`; do not merge `memo.md` into the big document; DOCX is not for reading — people read PDF and HTML.
- Human version: do not derive it by cutting codes out of the technical one; do not hand over `memo.md`/the technical report instead of the page; do not skip `humanizer-ru` over `readout.md` (the technical one is not cleaned with it — accuracy beats smoothness); do not drag verbatim quotes along — there goes the type of material (number, fact, study, position, product, case, norm, «found nothing»).

## Own measurement as a source (lesson of the 3d-meshes-code-rendered run, 2026-10-22)

The red team struck half the conclusions off the first measurement; so as not to repeat it:
- The script's raw output is written to `evidence/*.log` before the source card; «Key quotes» are taken from the log verbatim, not as a retelling.
- At least two separate processes and a different order of frames/inputs; the time — the median of ≥8 runs after warm-up, not a single value. The first run counts as reconnaissance.
- Versions (browser, library) and the renderer/environment string go into the log; without them «the same binary» is not asserted.
- If the system under test already contains a policy on the topic (flags, cache, backend pinning), `grep` it first: the recommendation must not contradict it.
- A `file://` source is not checked for liveness (`check_citations.py` marks it `LOCAL_FILE`); `finish.py` pulls in `requests` itself via `uv`.
