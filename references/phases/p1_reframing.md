## Phase 1. Reframing

**Model:** `opus` / `high` (see `model_routing.md`). The quality of the reframing determines the whole research — do not economise.

Goal: make sure we are searching for the right thing. Most bad research is bad questions, not bad search.

**Steps:**
1. Rewrite the question in your own words: «I understand the task like this: …». This is a check that the pictures match.
2. Assemble the **Decision Spec** (3 mandatory fields: decision verb+object+deadline / consumer→their next step / ≥1 if-then fork «will show X → I do A»). Not a single fork ⇒ an honest downgrade to shallow, «curiosity». Check `~/.claude/research/applications_ledger.csv` for a pattern of unapplied runs. Details — `question_reframing.md` item 2.
3. Formulate 2–4 falsifiable hypotheses. Not «study the topic» (that is not a hypothesis), but «X is better than Y on Z» (falsifiable).
4. Formulate the stop criteria. Without them research drifts into infinity.
5. If there is murkiness — ask ≤3 clarifying questions (the fifth triage criterion — the question «what will you do differently depending on the answer?» — is mandatory if the forks cannot be derived). If the user answers «I don't know» — proceed on an explicit assumption.

See `question_reframing.md` for templates and push-back scenarios.
