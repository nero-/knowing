## Fetch measurements

- 21.08.2026, 7 URLs: WebFetch did not get 6 of 7 (nytimes, theverge, wired, stackoverflow, reuters, reddit; only arxiv came through). `scripts/fetch_source.py` returned content at the first tier for three: nytimes 13 KB, theverge 36 KB, wired 42 KB. The other three — an auth wall: reuters 401, reddit 403 «log in or use your developer token», stackoverflow 403 Cloudflare.
- 24.08.2026: stackoverflow HTML — 403 Cloudflare, its Atom feed — 200 and 115 KB. The feed is generated by a separate pipeline and is usually not covered by the anti-bot layer; the same at WSJ, FT, Economist (articles behind a paywall, the feed open). The main finding of the measurement: the feed survives the HTML block.
